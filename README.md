## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Farmers depend on multiple intermediaries, such as traders, commission agents, wholesalers and retailers, to sell their agricultural products. Each intermediary adds a profit margin, reducing farmers' earnings while increasing prices for consumers. Lack of direct market access, price transparency, bargaining power and proper transportation facilities further affects the agricultural supply chain.

Benefits:

AgriConnect helps farmers increase their earnings by providing direct access to buyers and improving their bargaining power. Consumers benefit from fresher agricultural products and potentially lower prices. The platform also improves supply-chain transparency, reduces unnecessary transportation and helps minimise food wastage. Additionally, it provides the government with useful agricultural transaction data for better market monitoring and decision-making.

## Proposed Solution
AgriConnect – From Farm to Family

AgriConnect is an AI-powered digital marketplace that directly connects farmers with consumers, retailers and bulk buyers. It enables farmers to list their agricultural products, compare market prices and receive orders directly from buyers. The platform integrates AI-based price recommendations, online transactions, smart logistics and product traceability to reduce unnecessary intermediaries and ensure fair prices for both farmers and consumers.

## Technical Approach
The platform will be developed using React.js and Flutter for the frontend, Python FastAPI or Node.js for the backend, and PostgreSQL for database management. AI and machine learning techniques will analyse historical mandi prices, seasonal demand and market trends to recommend suitable selling prices. Firebase OTP will provide authentication, while UPI integration will support digital payments. Google Maps or OpenStreetMap will help manage transportation and delivery.

The system workflow begins with farmer registration and product listing, followed by AI-based price recommendations. Buyers can browse products, compare prices and place orders directly. Payments and delivery are then coordinated through the platform, ensuring a transparent transaction process.

## Feasibility and Viability
The solution is technically and economically feasible because it uses existing technologies, government agricultural datasets and digital payment infrastructure. It can initially be implemented in a single district and gradually expanded to other regions. Regional language support will make the platform accessible to farmers with limited digital knowledge. Partnerships with Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs), cooperatives and local transport providers will improve adoption and operational efficiency.

## Impact and Benefits
AgriConnect helps farmers increase their earnings by providing direct access to buyers and improving their bargaining power. Consumers benefit from fresher agricultural products and potentially lower prices. The platform also improves supply-chain transparency, reduces unnecessary transportation and helps minimise food wastage. Additionally, it provides the government with useful agricultural transaction data for better market monitoring and decision-making.

## Research and References
The proposed solution can utilise existing government initiatives and agricultural information systems such as e-NAM  for electronic agricultural trading, AGMARKNET  for mandi price information, and ONDC  for digital commerce infrastructure. Additional research can be obtained from the Ministry of Agriculture  and NITI Aayog  websites.
